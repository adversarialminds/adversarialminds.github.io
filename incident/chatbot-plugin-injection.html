Kill-chain analysis

Initial AccessIndirect (I)
Context-enrichment tools feed untrusted third-party content (e.g. customer reviews) to the chatbot without separating it from trusted content, enabling indirect injection.
these tools do not distinguish the website’s trusted content (e.g., product descriptions) from untrusted, third-party content (e.g., customer reviews), introducing a risk of indirect prompt injection Abstract
Priv. Esc.Instruction override (IO)
Plugins that fail to protect conversation-history integrity let attackers forge fake system messages, overriding the chatbot's instructions.
allowing adversaries to forge conversation histories (including fake system messages), boosting their ability to elicit unintended behavior Abstract
Recon.—not demonstrated
Persist.Dependent (channel)
Injected third-party content (e.g. a malicious review) is scraped into the chatbot's RAG knowledge base and retrieved again in later, benign user sessions.
Unlike direct prompt injections, indirect ones against web chatbots are persistent: once malicious content is embedded into the chatbot’s knowledge base, it can be retrieved later and trigger harmful behavior even during benign user interactions. arXiv 2511.05797, p7
